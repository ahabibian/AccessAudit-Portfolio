# CV Text — AccessAudit

## English

**AccessAudit — Accessibility Compliance & Evidence Platform**  
*Founder / Product & Software Engineer — Independent Project*

Designed and developed a desktop-first accessibility compliance platform covering automated scanning, structured evidence, manual-review workflows, remediation guidance, re-scan comparison, and professional customer reporting. Built deterministic reporting and evidence-preservation pipelines with clear separation between automated findings and cases requiring human assessment.

**Technology:** Python, Playwright, Chromium, axe-core, GitHub Actions, automated testing, and professional PDF reporting.  
**Portfolio:** https://github.com/ahabibian/AccessAudit-Portfolio
